Teacher Activities:

The teacher will write children rights and responsibilities

The teacher will write children rights and responsibilities on the chalkboard. The teacher will read the children’s rights and responsibilities to the learners, identify words that can be divided into three or more syllables. The teacher will also identify rhyming words.

Learner Activities:

Leaners will divide words into syllables. Identify rhyming words from the rights and reasonability abstract.

Leaners will sing a rhyme song about children’s rights and responsibilities

Assessment Strategy

Informal

Formal

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: They will break words into syllables and identify rhyming words.
  • Leaners are going cut and paste their rights and responsibilities from a worksheet.
  • Group 2: They will rewrite the rhyming words.
  • Leaners are required to do a chart and complete rights and responsibilities correctly.
  • Group3: Leaners will list the rhyming words.
  • Learners will be required to identify initial sounds and rhyming words in a picture story.
  • They must highlight initial sounds with green and rhyming words with red.
  • Breaking words into syllables using flash cards.
  • Do a role play about children’s rights and responsibilities.
