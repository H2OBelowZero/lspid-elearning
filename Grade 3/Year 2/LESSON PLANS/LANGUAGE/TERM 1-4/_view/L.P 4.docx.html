LESSON PLAN

Subject: English

Grade: 3 year 2

Term: 1

Week: 3-4

Topic: My feelings and emotions

Content: Reading and viewing

Time allocation: 5-6 week

Date Started:

Date Completed:

Prior content knowledge:

The teacher will ask learners to make face of when they are happy, sad, excited, angry and shocked.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

Recognize the face of someone who is happy, sad, excited, angry and shocked.

Able to read and make expressions of Happy, sad, excited, angry and shocked.

Vocabulary /Important word:

Recognize

Read

Teaching Strategies:

Demonstrate

Observation

Questions and Answers

Resources: smart board

Projector

Laptop

Charts

Teacher Activities: The teacher will play a slideshow on feelings and emotions. The slides will have expressions of people who are happy, sad, excited angry and shocked. Then the teacher will show learners a chart on emotion for learners to read and align the emotion with the correct expression of emotions.

Learner Activities: learners will watch the slide show and later answer questions. The learner will match column A of emotions with the correct picture of expressions on column B.
