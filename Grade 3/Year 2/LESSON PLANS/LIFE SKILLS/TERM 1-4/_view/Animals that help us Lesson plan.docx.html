Subject: Life Skills

Grade: 3 year 2

Term: 4

Week: 3-4

Topic: Animal creatures that help us.

Content: Beginning knowledge and personal social wellbeing.

Time allocation: 1 week

Date Started:

Date Completed:

Prior content knowledge:

Together with the learners the teacher will discuss things we get from animals and things domestic animals do to help us I our daily activities.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify and label animals that work for us.
  • Identify and label animal that give us food as well animals that help us with our household activities.
  • Able to mention the functions of animals that can help us.

Teacher Activities

The teacher will demonstrate to learners using pictures Animals that give us Food. (goats and cows give milk and beef, chickens give eggs and chicken meat, bees give us honey) Furthermore the teacher will also play a video to learners where the will how animals can help us make Clothes (Sheep give us wool, cows give us leather)

Identify animals that work for us:

The teacher will discuss the use of dogs in our households (Dogs as guide dogs, watch dogs, sniffer dogs) Donkeys and Horses as draught animals or for transport. Cows to pull ploughs and make fertilizer for the soil.

Learner Activities

Match Column A and Colum B of animals and their functions to the Human society.
