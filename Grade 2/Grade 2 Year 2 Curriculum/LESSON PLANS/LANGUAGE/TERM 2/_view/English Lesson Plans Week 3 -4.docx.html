TOPIC: Creature living in water: Ocean, River, Pond

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have prior knowledge about land animals

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Know different creatures that live in water
  • Identify freshwater animals
  • Identify saltwater animals
  • Know how they breathe
  • Identify animals that can live in and out of water (frogs; turtles, crabs)

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Shark
  • Dolphin
  • Crab
  • Lobster
  • Whale
  • Seahorse
  • Jellyfish
  • Octopus
  • Starfish, etc.

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered
  • Question and answer Demonstration

Resources:

  • Dangerous sea animals story: https://learnenglishk ids.britishcouncil.or g/en/short-stories/a ngel-look-out
  • Chalkboard
  • Animal world chart
  • Worksheet
  • Big storybook

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • I will introduce the lesson by playing a video about dangerous sea animals.
  • Ask, where do these animals live? Write ocean/sea on the board and ask learners to tell you the names of any animals they know that live in the sea.
  • Explain that World Oceans Day is 8 June and that people need to help keep our oceans clean for the animals that live in them.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners watch the video about the dangerous sea animals.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions and ask clarity seeking questions.

Phonemic Awareness

  • Revise previously taught sounds.
  • Teach different sounds at the end of your own name and friend’s names.
  • Segment sentences into individual words by clapping on each word.
  • Introduce ‘at’ family sounds;

cat c-a-t pat p-a-t

bat b-a-t Nat n-a-t

sat s-a-t vat v-a-t

rat r-a-t mat m-a-t hat h-a-t

  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read a Big Book about water animals with the learners and ask learners what the story was about.
  • Learners read Big Book stories with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace simple outlines of water animals pictures.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines ), letters and words to trace.
  • Write numbers 1 - 15.
  • Learners trace simple outlines of water animals and try to draw some water animals.
  • learners trace patterns, letter and words
  • Learners write 1 - 15.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to colour the pictures.
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to match words with pictures.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to draw and label pictures of each water animal.

Learners will be assigned an activity to paint the pictures of animals living in water.
