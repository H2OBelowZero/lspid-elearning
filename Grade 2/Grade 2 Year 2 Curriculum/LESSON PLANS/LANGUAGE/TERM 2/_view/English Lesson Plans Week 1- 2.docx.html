TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners watch the video about seasons of the year.
  • Learners share their experiences about seasons of the year.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions.

Listening and Speaking

  • I will introduce the lesson by playing a video about the seasons of the year.
  • Write the names of the seasons on the board. Ask learners to tell the class some things associated with the different seasons.
  • Explain to learners what happens in spring, summer, autumn and winter.
  • Discuss how seasons affect the type of clothes, food and outdoor activities.

Phonemic Awareness

  • Work on developing auditory sequential memory by repeatedly listening to ABC songs.
  • Teach the letter-sound (z) and other words that end in -zz.

Reading

  • Read a Big Book about seasons with the learners and ask learners what the story was about.
  • Sort pictures into the different seasons and give learners sentences to match with the pictures.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Teach the letter-sound (z) and words that end in -zz.
  • Give learners patterns, letters and words to trace.
  • Write numbers 1 - 15.
  • Learners write 1 - 15.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to colour the pictures.
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to match words with pictures.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to draw and label pictures of each season.
  • Learners to sing seasonal song
