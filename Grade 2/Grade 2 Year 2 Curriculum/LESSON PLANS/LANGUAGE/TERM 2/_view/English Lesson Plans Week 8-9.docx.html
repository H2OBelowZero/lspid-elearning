Subject: English

Grade: 2

Term: 2

Week: 8 - 9

TOPIC: Farm and wild animals

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have prior knowledge about land animals.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Introduce learners to different types of animals
  • The value of having a pet
  • How to take care and treat animals
  • Animals Welfare

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Horse
  • Sheep
  • Cow
  • Goat
  • Pig
  • Chicken

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered
  • Question and answer Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Videos
  • Animal world chart
  • Worksheet
  • Big Book 7

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • I will start the lesson by showing the animal world poster and listening to some animal stories.
  • Ask learners probing questions like where do the farm/wild animals live?
  • Discuss the difference between farm and wild animals; the economic purpose e.g., providing many products such as meat, dairy products, wools, leather, etc.
  • Teach learners Old MacDonald had a farm song/listen to it on the audio clip.

Phonics awareness

  • Give learners sets of rhyming words and distinguish aurally the ending sound.
  • Build and blend three-letter words.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners study animal world posters and listen to animal stories.
  • Learners attempt to answer probing questions.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions.
  • Learners listen to the lyrics and attempt to sing Old MacDonald had a farm song.

Assessment goal: Assessment strategy: Informal

Formal

Reading

  • Read Big Book 7 and 8 (Animals at sea, The wolf and the Seven Little Goats, The Hare and the Tortoise, Wendy Whale to the rescue) and other enlarged texts with the learners.

Handwriting and writing

  • Give each learner simple outlines of pictures, patterns and their own name to trace.
  • Give each learner an activity sheet and ask them to colour in the animals.
  • Write numbers 1 - 15.
  • Learners read Big Book stories with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.
  • Learners write a set of given activities in their workbooks.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to colour the pictures.
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to match words with pictures.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to name farm and wild animals.
  • Learners to sing Old MacDonald had a farm.
