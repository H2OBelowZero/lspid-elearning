Subject: English

Grade: 2

Term: 4

Week: 1-2

TOPIC: Water

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have prior knowledge about water and its uses.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Understand and discuss the water cycle.
  • Understand the importance of saving water.
  • Understand and discuss the water purification process.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Raindrops
  • Water cycle
  • Clouds
  • Water
  • Land
  • Sun
  • Condensation
  • Evaporation
  • Precipitation
  • Collection

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered.
  • Question and answer Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Animal world chart
  • Worksheet
  • Big storybook
  • Video
  • Paint
  • Cotton wool

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • Will introduce the lesson by playing a video about ‘water is everywhere’.
  • Ask, what do we use water for? Why is water important?
  • Explain the importance of water?

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners watch the video about water being everywhere.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions and ask clarity seeking questions.

Assessment goal: Assessment strategy: Informal

X

Formal

Assessment tool:

  • Rubric

Phonemic Awareness

  • Revise previously taught sounds.
  • Develop auditory discrimination.
  • Identify letter-sound relationship of single letters.
  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read a Big Book about water with the learners and ask learners what the story was about.
  • Learners read Big Book stories with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace simple outlines of water pictures.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines), letters and words to trace.
  • Draw or paint pictures.
  • Spell and write short words using phonic sounds learnt.
  • Learners trace simple outlines of water, clouds, the water cycle.
  • learners trace patterns, letter, and words.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: trace the water cycle.
  • Group 2: label pictures of the water cycle.
  • Group 3: Fill in the missing letters of the water cycle.

Create a large water cycle model
