Subject: English

TOPIC: Road/Traffic Safety

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have prior knowledge about crossing the road.
  • Learners use and see the road every day they come to school.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify Pedestrians
  • Identify Cyclists
  • Identify Passengers
  • Identify basic Road signs like Stop, Pedestrian crossing, Robot - the colors and the meaning of it.
  • Know how traffic officers help us

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Pedestrians
  • Cyclists
  • Passengers
  • Road signs
  • Robot
  • road

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered.
  • Question and answer
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Classwork books
  • Worksheets
  • Pictures
  • Poster
  • Video
  • Song

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • Introduce the lesson topic by playing rhyme video (Stop, look and listen, and before you cross the street).
  • Ask learners how they cross the street when they are at home, going to the shops or to school.
  • Teach the learners when you see a red Stop sign you must stop look around and wait until it is safe to proceed.
  • Teach learners about the safety use of buses/taxis as transport when coming to school

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners watch the rhyme video.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions and ask clarity seeking questions.

Phonemic Awareness

  • Recognize specific identified sight words from the lesson topic.
  • Build and blend words with the taught word families.
  • Recognize and name the letters of the alphabet.
  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read a story on the importance of road safety.
  • Learners read a story of road safety with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace simple outlines of important road sign pictures.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines), letters and words to trace.
  • Learners trace simple outlines of important road signs and try to draw some of them.
  • learners trace patterns, letter and words

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Color in a stop sign.
  • Group 2: Label the pictures.
  • Group 3: Fill in the missing letters activity.

Learners will play a game of ‘Crossing the Road’. Learners will line up on the road and use what they have learned about the rules of the road, to cross the road.
