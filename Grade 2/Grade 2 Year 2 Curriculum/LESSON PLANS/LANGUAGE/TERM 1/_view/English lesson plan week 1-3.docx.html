Subject: English

TOPIC: Me and my community

Content: Listening Speaking, Writing, Group guided reading

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners knowledge about looking after community such as no littering, no vandalism
  • Learners already know the importance of landing a hand to the community e.g. picking up papers

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Acknowledge that he/she is part of a community
  • Know the roles, rules and responsibilities in the community
  • Discuss ways how the community can work together to make it a nice place to live in
  • Know how to assist people in the community
  • Write their name

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Ambulance
  • Clinic
  • Doctor
  • Fire station
  • Hospital
  • Mall
  • Nurse
  • Police
  • Police station
  • Post office
  • Restaurants
  • Shops
  • Teachers

Teaching strategies:

  • Demonstration
  • Observation
  • Question and answer
  • Discussion
  • Group reading

Resources:

  • pictures
  • chalkboard
  • worksheet
  • youtube

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • teacher will show learners a poster of various parts of a community and ask learners what they see
  • teacher will explain to learners using pictures of children helping in a community and the results of helping
  • Teacher will ask learners what the role of different professions are such as the principal, teacher, nurse etc.
  • Then explain to learners how the different professions help the community
  • the teacher will then explain to learners the importance of respecting community members
  • engage in a group guided reading with the learners by reading simple picture books

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • learners will answer questions asked by the teacher
  • practice writing their names and alphabets
  • participate in a group guided reading

Practical task

  • Group 1: tell the teacher their name, sing the alphabets (listen to the alphabet song), sing letter sounds, complete drawing
  • Group 2: trace their names, sing the alphabets, fill in missing letters activity, filling in correct letters so that word matches the picture
  • Group 3: copy their name, copy the alphabets in their books, fill in missing letters activity, filling in correct letters so that word matches the picture, copy sentence

Learners will play a musical alphabet game
