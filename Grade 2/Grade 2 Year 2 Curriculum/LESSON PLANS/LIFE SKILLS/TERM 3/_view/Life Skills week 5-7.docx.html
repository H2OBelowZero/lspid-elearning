TOPIC: Animals and their shelters

Content: Personal and social wellbeing

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners already know that living things need a habitat or shelter to live in.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify animals and creatures that make their homes.
  • Identify animals and creatures that find their homes.
  • Identify animals and creatures that carry their homes.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Shelter
  • Animals
  • Creatures
  • Barns
  • Webs
  • Nests
  • Hives
  • Barrows and holes
  • shells
  • trees

  • Demonstration

discussion

Resources:

  • pictures
  • chalkboard
  • worksheet
  • video

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Teacher will explain to learners to the idea that living creatures besides themselves need their own space.
  • Teacher will explain to learners that animal homes come in a variety of shapes and sizes, and can be found anywhere.
  • Teacher will show learners some of the common animal homes and the animals that live in them.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Listen to the teacher’s explanations about living creatures or animals having their own home and that the homes come in different shapes and sizes.
  • Look and discuss the common animal homes and the animals that live in them.
