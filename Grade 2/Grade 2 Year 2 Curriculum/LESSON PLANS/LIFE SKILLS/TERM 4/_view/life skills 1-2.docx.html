TOPIC: Water

Content: Beginning Knowledge and Social Well-Being

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners have an idea about what is water.
  • Learners know where we find water, such as taps, rivers etc.
  • Learners know what water is used for.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Understand and discuss the water cycle.
  • Understand the importance of saving water.
  • Understand and discuss the water purification process.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Raindrops

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • To introduce the lesson topic, learners will watch a video, ‘water is everywhere’.
  • Teacher will make a chart, ‘Our Ideas About Water’, with questions such as what ideas do we have about water? Where do we find water? What can we do with it?
  • Learners will be asked the questions and their responses will be written on the chart.
  • Teacher will ask learners to gather around a water table or large tub with different containers.
  • Teacher will have learners explore the different things they can do with water and ask learners what can they do with water? What does the water look like in the container? What do you think it will look like if I pour it into another container? What is the same and what is different?
  • Teacher will play a video ‘After the rain.’
  • Tell a story about a raindrop's journey and then ask questions What does "cycle" mean? What shapes best represent cycles? Have you ever heard of the water cycle? What do you know about it?
  • Teacher will ask learners to create a very large water cycle model and each learner will make a portion of the water cycle.
  • The teacher will review the terms: condensation, evaporation, collection, and precipitation accompanied by a short video demonstrating each process respectively.
  • In conclusion, teacher will demonstrate using a video the water purification process.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch a video and tell the teacher what the video is about.
  • Learners will answer the questions asked by the teacher and their ideas will be written on the chart ‘Our Ideas About Water’.
  • Learners will gather around a water table or large tub of water with different containers, explore the different things they can do with water and answer the questions asked by the teacher.
  • Watch a video ‘After the rain’.
  • Listen to the story about a raindrops journey and answer the questions asked.
  • Create a large water cycle.
