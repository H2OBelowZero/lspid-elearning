Subject: Life Skills

Grade: 2

Term: 1

Week: 6-7

TOPIC: Me and other people

Content: Personal and Social well-being

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

Learners already know about people in their community

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Recognize own role as a child
  • Recognize roles of other people, at home, at school, in the community and in the environment
  • Deal positively with conflict

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Respect
  • Feelings
  • Politeness
  • kindness

Teaching strategies:

  • discussion
  • brainstorming
  • question and answer
  • demonstration

Resources:

  • worksheet
  • glue
  • chalkboard
  • poster

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • teacher will introduce the lesson by showing learners a clip on respect
  • teacher will ask learners what is respect and how do they respect people
  • teacher will then explain the lesson topic to learners about their roles as children and other people in their surroundings
  • teacher will demonstrate to learners different ways to deal positively with conflict

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • learners will watch a clip on respect
  • learners will answer the question about, what is respect and how do they respect people

  • Rubric

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: learners will watch a video on conflict resolution and will tell the teacher what they learned
  • Group 2: learners will receive a conflict scenario and will tell the teacher how to positively solve the conflict
  • Group 3: learners will complete a checklist on dealing with conflict

Learners will role play ways to interact with family and friends
