TOPIC: What do I need to live?

Content: Beginning Knowledge

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Knowledge about the different types
  • Learners are aware about sharing food and toys

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Different types of foods that help us to live
  • Things that we need in order to survive (water, sunlight, air)
  • Learners will be able to recognize different types of foods and how food helps people to live

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Shelter
  • Sunlight
  • Air
  • Water
  • Food

Teaching strategies:

  • Question and answer
  • Brainstorming
  • Learner centered

Resources:

  • Poster
  • Chalkboard
  • Worksheet
  • Glue
  • Scissors
  • Crayons

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The lesson will start by asking learners what they think will happen if they do not have food, water, air and shelter.
  • Teacher will show learners a poster of things that people need in order to survive such as food, water, air and shelter.
  • The teacher will explain to learners the needs and wants
  • The teacher will ask learners questions such as, can you live without air, water, food or shelter?
  • The teacher will ask learners why is it important to share, help and respect others.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will answer the questions asked by the teacher such as, “what will happen if they do not have food, water, air or shelter.
  • Learners will tell the teacher the importance of sharing, helping and respecting others.

Practical task

  • Group 1: trace a shelter, color a shelter, color a picture of the sun
  • Group 2: complete a worksheet by coloring the things we need in order to live, cut and paste activity, draw pictures of the things we need to survive, cut and paste things that we need to survive and things we do not need activity
  • Group 3: learners will cut and paste things that we need to live, cut and paste activity, write down five things we cannot survive without, arrange pictures to either needs or wants

Learners will create a simplified version of Maslow’s Hierarchy of gyms of needs to illustrate the things we need to live
