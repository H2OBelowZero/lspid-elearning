TOPIC: Senses

Content: Personal and Social Well-being

Date started:

Prior content knowledge:

Learners already have an idea what the five senses do

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Understand each of the five senses
  • Compare the means by which the senses gather information about the world
  • Explain how he/she cares for eyes, ears, nose, mouth and skin

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Senses
  • Sight
  • Hear
  • Smell
  • Touch
  • Taste

Teaching strategies:

  • Demonstration
  • Observation
  • Question and answer
  • Brainstorming

Resources:

  • Worksheet
  • Poster
  • Chalkboard
  • plastic
  • Sensory objects

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • teacher will go through the five senses with the learners by reviewing what each sense is about
  • teacher will play a song to introduce and reinforce senses

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will review what each sense is about
  • Learners will listen to a song that reinforces senses
