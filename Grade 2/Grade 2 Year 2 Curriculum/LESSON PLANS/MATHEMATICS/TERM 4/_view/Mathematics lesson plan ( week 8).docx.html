TOPIC: Space and shape

Content: 3D Shapes

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners have the prior knowledge of different shapes.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Recognize 3D shapes in the classroom and in pictures.
  • Identify the 3D shapes in the classroom and in pictures
  • Name the 3D shapes in the classroom and in the pictures

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Spheres (ball shapes)
  • Prisms (box shapes)
  • Cylinders

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Brainstorming
  • Demonstration
  • Question and answer session

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Video
  • Worksheets
  • Crayons
  • Workbooks

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will play a video about different 3D shapes for the learners.
  • The teacher will show learners the different 3D shapes on the chart.
  • The teachers will demonstrate how to draw the 3D shapes.
  • The teacher will describe and name the 3D shapes for the learners.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the video played by the teacher.
  • Learners will observe different 2D shapes from the chart.
  • Learners will observe how the teachers draws the shapes and attempt to draw them on the worksheets.
  • Learners will recall and recite the name of the 3D shapes.

Assessment goal: Learners will have knowledge about shapes and space.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will observe the video played by the teacher and recite the name of the 3D shapes.
  • Group 2: Learners will trace the 3D shapes on their workbooks and worksheets, while also shading the shapes.
  • Group 3: Learners will draw the different 3D shapes and name them.
