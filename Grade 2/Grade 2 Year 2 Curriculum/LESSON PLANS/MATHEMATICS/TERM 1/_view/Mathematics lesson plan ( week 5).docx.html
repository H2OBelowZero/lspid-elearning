TOPIC: Numbers, Operations and Relationships

Content: Comparing whole numbers

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners already have prior knowledge of whole numbers as in week 2 they were counting from 0-10.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Order numbers from the smallest to the biggest.
  • Recognize whole numbers
  • Compare whole numbers using big, small, more, less and equal.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Small
  • Big
  • Less
  • More
  • Equal
  • Order

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Question and answer session
  • Collaboration
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Workbooks
  • Worksheets
  • Video

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will write the numbers starting from 1-10 randomly and ask learners to arrange the numbers from the smallest to the biggest.
  • The teacher will play a video with rhyming songs about numbers from 1- 10.
  • The teacher will ask learners to compare numbers according to which one is big and which one is small.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will arrange the numbers from the smallest to the biggest starting from 1-10.
  • Learners will observe the video and sing along while moving their body.

Assessment goal: Learners will be able to arrange whole numbers from the smallest to the biggest.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will observe and listen to the song on the video about whole numbers, then sing along while counting and using their body parts to move (hands, feet and fingers), choose which blocks are less or more, trace and count number activity
  • Group 2: Learners will trace the whole numbers starting from 1- 10, complete a comparing numbers cut and paste activity, complete a fill in missing number activity, color the smallest number green and the biggest number orange activity
  • Group 3: Learners will rearrange numbers written randomly and order them from 1-10 starting from the smallest to the biggest, compare numbers activity, complete an activity which entails filling in missing numbers that come before other numbers, write numbers from smallest to biggest on the number line

Learners will be singing rhyming songs while moving their body and compare numbers on the chalkboard
