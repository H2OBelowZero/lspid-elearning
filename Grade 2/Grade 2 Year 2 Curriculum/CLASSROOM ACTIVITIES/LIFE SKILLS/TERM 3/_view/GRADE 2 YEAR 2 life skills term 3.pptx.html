    LESSON OBJECTIVES

    Guide and support learners to achieve their full physical, intellectual, personal, emotional and social potential.

    Teach learners to exercise their constitutional rights and responsibilities and to respect the rights of others.

    Guide learners to make informed and responsible decisions (with support) about their health and environment.

    Develop skills such as self-awareness, problem-solving, interpersonal relations, leadership, decision-making, and communication to the individual potential of the learner.

    Develop learners’ skills to respond to real-life situations and play an active and functional role

    in the economy and society.

  4. Slide 4

    ASSESSMENT GOALS

    TRANSPORTIdentify rail transport Identify and list types of air transport Identify and list types of water transport Compare and group the different types of transport Describe and discuss transport of the past and today
    ROAD TRAFFIC SAFETYIdentify, name and label basic Road signs like Stop, Pedestrian crossing, Robot - the colours and the meaning of it.
    PEOPLE WHO HELP US IN OUR COMMUNITYIdentify, name and label the people in the community that help us. Discuss and compare how different people help one another. Know and practice who to contact when help is needed
    FRUIT AND VEGETABLESIndicate and sort fruit from vegetable
