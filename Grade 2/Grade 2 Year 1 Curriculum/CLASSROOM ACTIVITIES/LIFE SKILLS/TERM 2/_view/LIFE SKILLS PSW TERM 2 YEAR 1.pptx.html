    The learner must be able to:

    Name four seasons

    Discuss how seasons affect our - clothes, food and activities

    Understand how seasons affect growing things - sowing, growing and harvesting

    Discuss how seasons affect animals - include farming e.g., sheep shearing, animal dipping; birds e.g. migration and nesting

    CREATURES LIVING IN WATER, OCEAN, RIVER AND POND

  16. Slide 16

    LESSONS OBJECTIVES

    The learner must be able to:

    Compare salt/fresh water

    Identify land animals (All animals done up to now)

    Identify water animals - River - e.g. fish, crocodile, hippo, crabs, Ponds and dams - e.g. fish, frog, dragonfly, Salt water - Sea - e.g. fish - shark, dolphin

    Compare characteristics of land animals with water animals

  17. Slide 17

    Compare salt and fresh water

    Identify and label land animals

    Identify and label water animals, e.g. fish/crocodile/ hippo/crabs/frog/dragonfly /shark/dolphin

    WEEK 5, 6 & 7

    Animals and their shelters

  26. Slide 26

    LESSON OBJECTIVES

    The learner must be able to:

    Identify animals and creatures that make their homes - such as birds, some bees, ants,spiders etc.

    Identify animals and creatures that find their homes - such as baboons, snakes, squirrels etc.

    Identify animals and creatures that carry their homes - such as snails, tortoises etc.
