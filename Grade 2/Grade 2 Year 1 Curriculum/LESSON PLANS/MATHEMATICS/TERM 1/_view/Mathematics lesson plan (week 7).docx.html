TOPIC: Space and shape

Content: Features of shapes

Date started:

Prior content knowledge:

Learners already have the prior knowledge of different 2D shapes and different colours.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Compare the different 2D shapes in terms of colour and size.
  • Draw 2D shapes like the square, triangle, rectangle, and circle.
  • Sort the shapes in terms of colour and size.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Square
  • Rectangle
  • Circle
  • Triangle

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Question and answer session
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Charts
  • Crayons
  • Worksheets
  • Workbooks
  • Video

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will play a video that shows different 2D shapes with different colours.
  • The teacher will draw different 2D shapes on the chalkboard.
  • The teacher will ask the learners to look out for different shapes they can find in the classroom, for example: the windows have a square shape.
  • The teacher will read out the names of the shapes for the learners.
  • The teacher will give learners worksheets to complete.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will watch the video on the projector.
  • Learners will observe how to draw the different 2D shapes.
  • Learners will point out the furniture and describe the shape of the furniture in the classroom.
  • Learners will listen to the teacher as she reads out the names of the 2D shapes and recall them.

Assessment goal: Learners will have the knowledge about space and shape.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Trace the names of different shapes and colour the shapes.
  • Group 2: Draw different shapes with different sizes and colour them.
  • Group 3: Write down the names of the different shapes and compare those shapes to the furniture inside the classroom.

Learners will shade the shapes from their worksheets using different colours.
