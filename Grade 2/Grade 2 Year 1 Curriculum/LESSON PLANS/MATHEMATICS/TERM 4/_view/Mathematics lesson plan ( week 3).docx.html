Subject: Mathematics

Grade: 2

Term: 4

Week: 3

TOPIC: Number operations and relationship

Content: Describe, compare and order numbers 1- 20.

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have the prior knowledge with counting numbers.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Order numbers 1-20 from the smallest to the biggest.
  • Write numbers from 1 up to 20.
  • Compare numbers from 1 up to 20 using smaller than, greater than, more than, less than and is equal to.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Count
  • Write
  • Trace
  • Counters
  • More
  • Less
  • Big
  • Small
  • Equal to

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Question and answer session
  • Demonstrating
  • Collaboration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Classwork books
  • Worksheets
  • Videos

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will call learners individually to take out 20 Legos (counting objects) from the plastic.
  • The teacher will demonstrate to the learners how to write the number symbols on the chalkboard.
  • The teacher will play a mathematics educational video that will show on the projector the number symbols and how they are pronounced
  • The teacher will give learners worksheets where they will write down the number symbols on their own.
  • Thereafter the teacher will demonstrate the different comparison signs on the chalkboard and explain to the learners how they are used.
  • Then the teacher will give learners worksheets that they will complete.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The learners will take out 20 Legos from the plastic as instructed by the teacher to do so.
  • The learners will observe on the chalkboard how to write the number symbols and also how to use comparison signs.
  • Learners will complete an activity on the given worksheets.

Assessment goal: Learners will know how to count and write number symbols from 1-20.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity where they will be required to write down the number symbols on their books.
  • Group 2: Learners will be required to count using Legos and take out 20 Legos from the plastic.
  • Group 3: Learners will sing while counting, using a video played by the teacher and they will use their bodies like clapping hands and stamping their feet.

Learners will count using rhyme songs from the video played by the teacher, and they will also move their bodies while singing. Learners will also do a practical activity by counting the counters or Legos.
