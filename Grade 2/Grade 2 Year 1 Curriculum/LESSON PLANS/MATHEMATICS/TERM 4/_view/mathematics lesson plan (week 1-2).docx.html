TOPIC: Numbers, Operations and relationships

Content: Counting with whole numbers

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners have knowledge about numbers as they are exposed to them in their real lives.
  • I will evoke learners’ prior knowledge using the question and answer session where learners will be involved in the lesson.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify and recognize numbers from 1-50.
  • Count everyday objects reliable.
  • Identify numbers on the number chart or on the chalkboard.
  • Write number symbols from 1-20.
  • Read number names from 1-5.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Count
  • Whole numbers
  • Objects
  • Number chart
  • Identify
  • Recognize
  • Read

Teaching strategies:

  • Question and answer session
  • Brainstorming
  • Learner centered approach
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Number chart
  • Worksheets
  • Video
  • Counting objects (Legos)

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Will teach learners how to count by creating a rhythmic song about numbers.
  • Will ask learners to count using the number chart.
  • Will ask learners to identify numbers randomly on the number chart.
  • Will call learners individually to count the concrete objects (Legos) on the teacher’s table.
  • Learners will watch a video of rhyming songs about numbers.
  • Will write numbers on the chalkboard and ask learners to read those numbers.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will first listen to the teacher while she leads them on the rhyming song about numbers.
  • Thereafter, learners will imitate the rhyming song about numbers.
  • Learners will count using the number chart.
  • Learners will identify the numbers required by the teacher on the number chart.
  • Learners will count concrete objects on the teacher’s table.
  • Learners will watch a video on the smart board and sing along.
  • Learners will read the numbers written on the chalkboard.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will count using rhyming songs while moving their bodies.
  • Group 2: Learners will count using concrete objects (Legos), trace numbers on their worksheets and also color in numbers.
  • Group 3: Learners will count numbers from 1-50, write down the numbers on their worksheets and also fill in the missing numbers on their worksheets.
