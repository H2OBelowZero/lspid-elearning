Content: South African coins and bank notes

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners already have knowledge about the South African coins and bank notes.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify the South African coins and bank notes.
  • Recognize the South African coins and bank notes.
  • Add coins and bank notes.
  • Subtract coins and bank notes.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Add
  • Subtract
  • Coin
  • Bank note
  • Minus
  • Plus

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Brainstorming
  • Collaboration
  • Demonstrating
  • Question and answer session.

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Video
  • Worksheets
  • Charts
  • Coins
  • Bank notes

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will play a video to introduce learners to different coins and bank notes that we have in South Africa.
  • The teacher will come with the coins and bank notes and ask the learners to state how much she is holding.
  • The teacher will give learners worksheets where they will be required to add different coins and bank notes, and also include subtraction problems.
  • The teacher will walk around to check if learners are on the right track and scaffold learning.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch a video to see the different bank notes and coins.
  • Learners will answer the questions posed by the teacher regarding how much she is holding in her hand.
  • Learners will complete the worksheets and calculate using addition and subtraction.

Assessment goal:

Assessment strategy: Learners will have knowledge about addition and subtraction. Have knowledge about bank notes and coins.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners are going to watch the video played by the teacher, to gain knowledge about different coins and bank notes used in South Africa.
  • Group 2: Learners will answer the questions posed by the teacher verbally.
  • Group 3: Learners will complete the worksheets given to them and solve problems.

Learners will observe the bank notes and the coins brought by the teacher and try to combine or add the coins and the bank notes practically.
