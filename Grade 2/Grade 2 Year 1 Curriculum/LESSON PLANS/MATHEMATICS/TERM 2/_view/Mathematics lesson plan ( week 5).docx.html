TOPIC: Space and shape

Content: Language of position

Time allocation:

Date started: 08/05/23

Date completed: 12/05/23

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners have the prior knowledge of different positions regarding objects, and mother tongue language can be used in this instance to understand what learners already know.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Recognize the different positions of objects.
  • Identify the positions of those objects.
  • Demonstrate how different positions are formed.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • On top of
  • In front of
  • Behind
  • Up
  • Down
  • Next to
  • Between

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Brainstorming
  • Demonstration
  • Question and answer session

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Video
  • Worksheets
  • Crayons
  • Workbooks
  • Projector (slides)

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will play a video that demonstrates different positions of objects.
  • The teacher will show learners the different positions of positions of objects on the projector.
  • The teachers will ask learners to demonstrate the different positions of objects they can see inside the classroom.
  • The teacher will give learners worksheets to complete.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the video played by the teacher.
  • Learners will observe different positions of objects from the projector.
  • Learners will demonstrate the different positions of objects they can see inside the classroom.
  • Learners will complete the worksheets given by the teacher.

Assessment goal: Learners will have knowledge about shapes and space.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will observe the video played by the teacher and observe how different positions are formed.
  • Group 2: Learners will state whether the object is on top of, in front of, behind, up, down or next to.
  • Group 3: Learners will demonstrate how different positions are formed using different objects in the classroom, then complete the worksheets.
