Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have the prior knowledge with counting numbers.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Count forward in 1’s.
  • Count forward in 5’s.
  • Count forward in 10’s.
  • Arrange the sequence.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Count
  • Forward
  • Sequence
  • Arrange

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method.
  • Question and answer session
  • Demonstrating
  • Collaboration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Classwork books
  • Worksheets
  • Videos

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The teacher will play a video of a song demonstrating the sequence of numbers.
  • The teacher will write down the sequence of 1’s, 5’s, and 10’s on the board.
  • Thereafter the teacher will ask learners to recite the sequences on the board.
  • The teacher will give learners worksheets to complete where they will fill in the missing numbers in a sequence.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • The learners will watch a video demonstrated by the teacher on the projector.
  • The learners will recite the sequence of 1’s, 5’s, and 10’s written by the teacher on the board.
  • Learners will complete the worksheets where they will fill in the missing numbers in a sequence.

Assessment goal: Learners will know how to count and write number symbols from 1-5.

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will sing and recite the sequences that will be played in the video by the teacher.
  • Group 2: Learners will be required to recite the sequences written on the board by the teacher and count the Legos grouped in sequences.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity where they will be required to write fill in the missing numbers in a sequence and arrange different sequences.

Learners will count using rhyme songs from the video played by the teacher, and they will also move their bodies while singing. Learners will also do a practical activity by counting the counters or Legos grouped in sequences.
