TOPIC: Creatures living in water, ocean, river and pond

Content: Personal and Social wellbeing

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners are already familiar or at least have heard about different animals living in water and land.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify land animals
  • Identify water animals
  • Compare characteristics of land animals with water animals

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Creatures
  • Water
  • Ocean
  • River
  • Pond
  • Land
  • Habitat

Teaching strategies:

  • Demonstration
  • Observation
  • Question and answer
  • discussion

Resources:

  • pictures
  • chalkboard
  • worksheet
  • video

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Teacher will ask learners to name animals that live on land and explain to learners why they live on land.
  • Teacher will ask learners to name animals that live in water and explain to learners why they live in water.
  • Teacher will differentiate the characteristics of land animals with water animals
  • Teacher will ask learners what are the characteristics of land animals and water animals.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will name animals that live on land.
  • Learners will name animals that live in water.
  • Learners will tell the teacher the characteristics of land animals and water animals.
