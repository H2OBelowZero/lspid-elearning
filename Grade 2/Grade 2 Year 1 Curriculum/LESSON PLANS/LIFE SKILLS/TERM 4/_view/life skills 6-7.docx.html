TOPIC: Communication in Our World

Content: Beginning Knowledge and Social Well-being

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

Learners should have prior knowledge about communications they use daily, such as a cellphone.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify common communication skills.
  • Identify common communication technologies.
  • Identify ways that a person’s privacy and reputation may be compromised online.
  • Identify examples of texting style language, including abbreviations and emoticons.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Communication skills
  • Communication technologies
  • Privacy
  • Reputation
  • Online
  • Texting style language
  • Emoticons
  • abbreviations

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered.
  • Question and answer
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • chart
  • Worksheet
  • Paint
  • Cotton wool

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Introduce the lesson by playing a video on how people communicate daily. Ask learners to identify the different communication tools.
  • Show learners different tools for communication and how they are used.
  • Explain using examples to learners the different kinds of communication skills such as written communication, oral communication, non-verbal and visual communication etc.
  • Explain to learners the importance of online safety and how a person’s privacy can be compromised.
  • Show and explain to learner's examples of texting style language including abbreviations and emoticons.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the video on how people communicate daily.
  • Learner's name different communication tools and how they are used.
  • Learners will give examples of the different kinds of communication skills.
  • Learners will identify ways that a person’s privacy and reputation may be compromised online.
  • Learners will identify examples of texting style language (abbreviations and emoticons).
