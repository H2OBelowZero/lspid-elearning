TOPIC: Life at Night

Content: Beginning Knowledge and Social Well-being

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

Learners should have prior knowledge about what they do at night.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Discuss things they do at night- get ready for bed, read and tell stories, sleep and dream.
  • Identify people who work at night – such as security officers, doctors, pilots, truck drivers.
  • Identify night animals such as owls, hamsters, porcupines, leopards, jackals.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Night
  • Day
  • Moon
  • Stars
  • Sleep
  • Dream
  • Bed
  • Night shift
  • Night animals

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered.
  • Question and answer
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • chart
  • Worksheet
  • Paint
  • Cotton wool

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Introduce the lesson by playing a video of what children do at night. Ask learners what they do at night.
  • Ask learners which people work at night and why do they work at night. Explain to learners why such people work at night.
  • Ask learners to identify animals that are active at night. Why are these animals active at night?

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the nighttime video and tell the teacher what they do at night.
  • Learners identify people that work at night.
  • Learners identify night animals.

Practical task

Learners will create a poster that will differentiate between day and nighttime.

  • Group 1: Color a picture of a sleeping person.
  • Group 2: Color and cut out the pictures and paste them in the correct order.
  • Group 3: Cut out the picture and paste under day or night activity.
