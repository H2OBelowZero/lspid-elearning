TOPIC: Our Country South Africa

Content: Beginning Knowledge and Social Well-being

Time allocation:

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

Learners should have prior knowledge about the country they live in.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Know the name of our country South Africa
  • Recognize the map of South Africa
  • Recognize the symbols of South Africa
  • Name own province
  • Name own town
  • Recognize the South African flag and places where we can see it flying, and the meaning of colors.
  • Listen/hum/sing South African anthem

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Country
  • Province
  • City
  • Town
  • Flag
  • Colors
  • Anthem
  • Map

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered.
  • Question and answer
  • Demonstration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • chart
  • Worksheet
  • Paint
  • Cotton wool

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Introduce the lesson by playing the national anthem and ask learners to sing along.
  • Show learners a map of different countries and ask them which one is the map of South Africa.
  • Show learners the map of South Africa and explain what we find in the map such as provinces, cities, and towns.
  • Show learners the chart with the various national symbols and ask the learners to identify the pictures in the chart.
  • Explain the different colors on the South African flag and their meaning. Ask learners what the colors mean.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will listen and sing along to the national anthem.
  • Leaners look at the different maps and try to identify the map of South Africa.
  • Learners identify the symbols on the chart.
  • Learners tell the teacher what the colors on the South African map mean.

Practical task

Learners will draw/trace the South African symbols. Paint and create a chart of the symbols.

  • Group 1: Color in the national symbol
  • Group 2: Learners cut out the squares of a South African map and put them in the correct spaces and color it in.
  • Group 3: Answer the question by matching it with a picture.
