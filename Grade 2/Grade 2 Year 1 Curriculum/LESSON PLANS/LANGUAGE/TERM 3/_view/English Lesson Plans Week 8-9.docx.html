Subject: English

Grade: 2

Term: 3

Week: 8-9

TOPIC: Fruits and vegetables

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners have prior knowledge about fruits and vegetables.
  • They see fruits and vegetables at their homes, at school or at the streets.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Name different fruits that they know.
  • Name different vegetables that they know.
  • Explain the importance of fruits and vegetables in our bodies.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Fruits
  • Vegetables
  • Healthy living

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Question and answer session
  • Demonstrating
  • Collaboration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Fruits and vegetables chart
  • Worksheets
  • Storybook

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • I will introduce the lesson by playing a video with rhyming songs about fruits and vegetables.
  • I will ask learners to name different fruits and vegetables that they know.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the video about fruits and vegetables, then sing along.
  • Learners will name different fruits and vegetables that they know.

Assessment goal: Assessment strategy: Informal

X

Formal

Assessment tool:

  • Rubric

Phonemic Awareness

  • Recognize specific identified sight words from the lesson topic.
  • Build and blend words with the taught word families.
  • Recognize and name the letters of the alphabet.
  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read a story about the importance of healthy living and eating fruits and vegetables.
  • Learners read the story with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace the names of the fruits and vegetables.
  • Learners will write down the names of the fruits and vegetables.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines), letters and words to trace.
  • Learners will trace the names the names of the fruits and vegetables.
  • Learners will write down the names of the fruits and vegetables.
  • Learners trace patterns, letters and words.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to color the pictures of fruits and vegetables
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to write down the names of the fruits and vegetables which are written by the teacher on the board.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to draw a picture of any fruit or vegetable that they like.

Learners will draw and color different fruits of their choice.
