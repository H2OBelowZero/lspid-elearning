Listening and speaking

  • Chalkboard
  • Animal world chart
  • Worksheet
  • Big storybook
  • Introduce the lesson by asking learners to think about what kinds of things animals might need to survive.
  • Ask learners to think about some of the different kinds of habitats in the world by naming some of the most common (desert, forest, ocean, jungle).
  • Explain to learners that animals, just like people, need somewhere to live. Some animals prefer to build their own home. Some animals will look for a home that is ready for them to live in while others have a home that they carry around with them. Their home protects them from predators and from the weather and gives them a place to raise their babies.
  • Learners watch the video about the animal habitat.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions and ask clarity seeking questions.

Phonemic Awareness

  • Revise previously taught sounds.
  • Teach different sounds at the end of your own name and friend’s names.
  • Segment sentences into individual words by clapping on each word.
  • Introduce ‘an’ family sound;

man m-a-n van v-a-n pan p-a-n tan t-a-n can c-a-n ban b-a-n ran r-a-n fan f-a-n

  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read aloud the book Habitats by William

B. Rice.

  • Pause as you read to consider unknown words found in the text. Ask learners, "What do they think will happen next? Why do you think that?"

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace simple outlines of animal habitat.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines ), letters and words to trace.
  • Write numbers 1 - 15.
  • Learners read Big Book stories with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.
  • Learners trace simple outlines of animal habitat.
  • Learners trace patterns, letter and words
  • Learners write 1 - 15.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to colour the pictures.
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to match words with pictures.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to draw and label pictures of each animal habitat.

Learners will be directed to make a home of their favorite animal.
