Subject: English

Grade: 2

Term: 4

Week: 6-7

TOPIC: Communication in our world

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners have prior knowledge about how they communicate with their families, friends’, peers or people around them. They use gadgets such as cellphones to communicate with those far from them.

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify common communication skills.
  • Identify common communication technologies.
  • Identify ways that a person’s privacy and reputation may be compromised online.
  • Identify examples of texting style language, including abbreviations and emoticons.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Communication
  • Technology
  • Texting
  • Privacy
  • Reputation

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Question and answer session
  • Demonstrating
  • Collaboration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Chart
  • Worksheet
  • Story book
  • Projector

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • I will introduce the lesson by playing a video about the different kinds of communication that people use on a daily basis, e.g. cellphone, laptop etc.
  • I will ask learners to explain the importance of technology especially through communication and how this gadgets help them.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the video about different kinds of communication that people use on a daily basis.
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions and ask clarity seeking questions.

Assessment goal: Assessment strategy: Informal

X

Formal

Assessment tool:

  • Rubric

Phonemic Awareness

  • Recognize specific identified sight words from the lesson topic.
  • Build and blend words with the taught word families.
  • Recognize and name the letters of the alphabet.
  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read a story about the importance of communication.
  • Learners will read the story about the importance of communication together with the teacher.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace the names of different gadgets used for communication.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines), letters and words to trace.
  • Learners will trace the names of different gadgets used for communication.
  • Learners trace patterns, letters and words.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to color the pictures of different gadgets.
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to trace the pictures of different gadgets.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to draw a picture of a gadget of their choice.

Learners will draw and color any picture of a gadget used for communication.
