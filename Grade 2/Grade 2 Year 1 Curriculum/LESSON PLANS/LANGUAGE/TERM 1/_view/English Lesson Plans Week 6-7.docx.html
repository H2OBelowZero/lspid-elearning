Subject: English

Grade: 2

Term: 1

Week: 6-7

TOPIC: Me and other people

Content:

  • Listening and Speaking
  • Phonological/Phonemic Awareness/Phonics
  • Emergent Reading
  • Shared Reading
  • Handwriting and Writing

Time allocation:

2 hours daily

Date started:

Date completed:

Prior content knowledge:

  • Learners should have prior knowledge about the who she/he is
  • Qualities of a good friend and be aware of his/her surroundings.
  • Learners interact with the people who help the community e.g., at schools, hospitals etc

Lesson objectives: The learners must be able to:

  • Identify different people around them, boys and girls
  • The learner should be able to differentiate between elders and his/her peers.
  • Know the importance of friendship and how to make friends.

  • Identify different people who help us with different services in our communities.
  • State the services provided by the people in our communities.
  • Explain how the services work.
  • Explain how the services help or benefit the community
  • How to positively deal with conflict and bullying that includes self-esteem.

Vocabulary/ important words:

  • Elders
  • Friends
  • People
  • Bullying
  • Self esteem
  • Environment
  • Gender
  • Race
  • Conflict

Teaching strategies:

  • Learner centered method
  • Question and answer session
  • Demonstrating
  • Collaboration

Resources:

  • Chalkboard
  • Chart
  • Worksheet
  • Big storybook
  • Projector

TEACHER’S ACTIVITIES

Listening and Speaking

  • The teacher will introduce the lesson by playing a video about different people who offer important services in our communities.
  • The teacher will then ask the learners to explain if they are self-aware, and know who they are. Considering their gender.
  • The teacher will also discuss how the learners can deal with low self esteem and handle conflict with their peers.

LEARNER’S ACTIVITIES

  • Learners will watch the video about different people such s gender and race
  • Learners actively participate in the class discussions and ask clarity seeking questions.

Phonemic Awareness

  • Recognize specific identified sight words from the lesson topic.
  • Build and blend words with the taught word families.
  • Recognize and name the letters of the alphabet.
  • Learners actively listen to the ABC songs and try to identify letters of their own names.
  • Learners attempt to answer given activities.

Reading

  • Read a story about the importance of knowing who you are as a child.your place of birth and the importance of respect.
  • Learners read Big Book stories with the teacher. Predict what will happen next in the story using pictorial cues.

Handwriting and Writing

  • Task learners to trace the names and the occupations of the people who help us in our communities.
  • Give learners patterns (rounded, pointed, vertical lines), letters and words to trace.
  • Learners will trace the names and the occupations of the people who help us in our communities.
  • Learners trace patterns, and letters.

Differentiation (Enrichment opportunities/ addressing barriers)

Practical task

  • Group 1: Learners will be given an activity to color the pictures of a certain profession/career e.g. Doctor.
  • Group 2: learners will be given an activity to match the pictures of different people and the occupations they work in.
  • Group 3: Learners will be given an activity to draw a picture of a profession/career that they like.

Learners will draw and color different pictures of the careers of their choice.
